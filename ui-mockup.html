The gate, every time you re-open

3 Share sheet · PRIMARY

The daily move: “Share → Vault”

Long-press an image anywhere → Share → Vault. This bottom sheet appears, you optionally tap a tag, hit Save, it’s gone. No biometric prompt — public-key wrapping means saving never needs auth. This is the flow to get perfect.

Your call

  • A
    How prominent should the source be? Full row with the raw android-app:// id (shown), or just a quiet “From WhatsApp” line?
  • B
    One-tap-save, or always confirm? Could auto-save on share and show only an undo toast — fastest, but no tag-at-save moment.
  • C
    How many quick-tag chips? Spec says top 6 by use. Fewer = calmer sheet; more = less “+ new” typing.
  • D
    Save button wording: “Save” / “Save to Vault” / “Lock it away”?

4 Full-screen viewer

One item, decrypted only in memory

Tap a tile to open it. Swipe between items; pinch to zoom. A pull-up drawer carries the metadata — source, when you saved it, tags. Never written to a temp file, and screenshots are blocked here too.

Your call

  • A
    Metadata: always-visible drawer, or hidden until you swipe up? Mockup shows it docked. Hiding it gives the photo the full screen.
  • B
    Show the source URL/domain here? Handy for “where’s this from,” but it’s the most sensitive field — see the spec’s DB-leak note.
  • C
    Actions on an item: which do you want reachable — retag, add to album, export this one, delete?

5 Import

Pulling media in — and out of your gallery

Your own picker (not Android’s — the system one can’t delete originals). Multi-select, then one system dialog deletes them all. The second tab is the folder import from your note: point it at a directory, it encrypts copies in, no plaintext left behind for the stock gallery to scan.

Your call

  • A
    “Photos & videos” and “From a folder” as two tabs, or one merged flow? Mockup splits them.
  • B
    Delete-originals: default on or off? On = true vault hygiene. Off = safer while you trust it. You always get the final system confirm either way.
  • C
    After import, jump to the grid or stay to import more?
